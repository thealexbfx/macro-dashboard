# MacroLens Phase 2.2 — Dataset integrity audit

Audit date: 24 October 2026. Scope: every dataset exposed by the isolated V2 catalogue (13 existing plus the two NSA CPI definitions required for correct headlines), and an inventory of nine additional upstream research registrations. No production provider, Streamlit application, hosting, credential or production registry changed. This is a dated check of latest revised observations, not a certification of every historical observation or future release.

## Findings and corrections

- Payrolls previously presented the employment level, 159,044 thousand people, as the primary indicator. September monthly jobs are `(159044 − 159015) × 1000 = +29,000`. Today now shows jobs; Explorer and original-value Investigate retain the level.
- CPI previously presented a raw SA index as the primary inflation indicator. Today now uses CPIAUCNS annual NSA inflation: `(336.940 / 324.800 − 1) × 100 = 3.737684729%`, displayed as 3.7%. SA monthly and alternative transformations remain available.
- GDP reference-quarter age incorrectly produced a stale warning for Q2. The September 30 third estimate is still the latest release; Q3 advance is due October 29. Freshness now uses the dated release calendar when known.
- FEDFUNDS was insufficiently explicit: September 3.75% is a monthly average effective rate, not the FOMC target or daily EFFR. The label now states this directly. Treasury changes remain basis points and explicitly are not bond returns.
- API success did not establish independent price verification. Four Yahoo closes now explicitly remain Unverified. DXY historical close 101.8099975586 differs from regular-market quote 101.752; these fields have different timing and are no longer conflated.
- Missing calendar periods, malformed ordering/duplicates, unit conflicts and wrong Yahoo identities lacked sufficient explicit safeguards. Payloads now fail validation or expose warnings, without filling gaps or deleting legitimate shocks. Calculation logic for headlines and widget movement is centralized in the backend.

## Vintage, missing observations and calculations

- BLS Employment Situation, October 2: September +29,000, August revised +133,000 and July revised −10,000. Current retrieved PAYEMS reproduces the September headline at this vintage; no material discrepancy. Earlier published August +162,000 / July +21,000 differ because of revisions. Two prior months were revised down by a combined 60,000. [BLS report](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_10022026.htm).
- CPI September release October 14: annual NSA headline 3.7%, core 2.6%; SA monthly changes 0.6% / 0.3%. Annual raw calculations 3.737684729% / 2.564358351%, monthly 0.648548025% / 0.334848193%, match at publication precision. October 2025 is missing in current CPI histories. Annual change looks up the same calendar month twelve months earlier, never twelve available rows earlier. Missing comparison periods remain unavailable. [BLS CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm).
- PCE August release September 30: annual headline/core 3.4% / 3.0%; calculated 3.419031825% / 3.007595976% from SA price indices, not annualized monthly changes. Raw index levels were checked against FRED, published rounded rates against BEA. The annual update revises history beginning January 2021. [BEA PCE](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-august-2026).
- GDP Q2 2.2% is already quarterly annualized growth, not a GDP level or year-over-year rate. Do not annualize it again. [BEA GDP and schedule](https://www.bea.gov/data/gdp/gross-domestic-product).
- H.15 October 23 publication reports October 22 10-year yield 4.96%, versus October 21 5.13%: −0.17 percentage points = −17 bp. Observation day and publication day are separate. Daily EFFR 3.88% in H.15 is not September FEDFUNDS monthly average 3.75%. [Federal Reserve H.15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/). The [September FOMC target](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm) was 3.75–4.00%; no target series has been substituted for FEDFUNDS.
- Revision counts compare overlapping cache observations only. Zero does not imply unrevised history. No point-in-time vintage archive is available. Source retrieval time is separate from the reference period and any documented release date. Newly arriving vintages do not inherit dated verification automatically.
- Discontinuity warnings retain observations, including economic shocks. Invalid refreshes retain the prior cache only with stale status and its original retrieval timestamp. Cold failures are unavailable. No interpolation, forward filling, synthetic replacement or alternative instrument fallback is introduced.
