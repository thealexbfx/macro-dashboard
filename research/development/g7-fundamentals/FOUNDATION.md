## Next batches and economy-specific priorities

1. **Canada and Japan backfills.** Validate full StatCan CPI tables and Japan’s 2025-base historical files. Then acquire Canada LFS, GDP, household credit and mortgage data. Compare debt service, mortgage renewals, employment and housing to track household transmission. Pair oil production and trade as external exposures. [BoC household analysis](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/publications/financial-stability-report/financial-stability-report-2026/households/) · [BoJ outlook](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/outlook/highlight/ten202607.htm)
2. **US and UK parity and gaps.** US PCE and retail alongside payrolls, investment, credit and housing distinguish demand strength from monetary transmission. UK services inflation, real earnings, household credit and housing help track demand and rate pass-through. Add output per hour and business investment to monitor structural weakness. Preserve SA and NSA variants separately. [BEA national accounts](https://www.bea.gov/data/gdp/gross-domestic-product) · [UK Article IV](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2026/172/article-A001-en.xml)

France household spending, services confidence, employment and public finances separate domestic demand from fiscal drag. Italy debt servicing and maturity structure, benchmark yields, bank credit and productivity track refinancing and growth. Retain harmonised GDP, HICP, labour and fiscal comparators separately. [Destatis production](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Economic-Sectors-Enterprises/Industry-Manufacturing/_node.html) · [INSEE series](https://www.insee.fr/en/statistiques/series) · [Banca d’Italia statistical database](https://infostat.bancaditalia.it/)
