# G7 official data source map

Producer and retrieval route are separate catalog fields. The CSV distinguishes registered IDs from research selections. Some source families below are leads requiring exact series resolution, release-calendar checks and rights evidence.

| Country | Prices, growth and labour | Demand, production, housing and confidence | Monetary, credit and fiscal | Trade, energy and population |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | BLS CPI/PPI, CES/CPS/JOLTS and productivity. BEA NIPA GDP, PCE, corporate profits | Census MRTS retail, M3 orders, construction spending, permits and starts. Fed G.17 output and capacity. Michigan sentiment requires review | Fed H.15/H.4.1/H.6/H.8, NY Fed EFFR/SOFR, Treasury curve and FiscalData. FHFA HPI as a separate house-price concept | Census trade, BEA international transactions, EIA petroleum/gas/electricity and Census population |
| United Kingdom | ONS CPI/CPIH/RPI, GDP and national accounts, labour and earnings, productivity and unit labour costs | ONS retail volumes, Index of Production, output in construction, HM Land Registry UK HPI, MHCLG housing supply. GfK confidence is a third-party lead | BoE Bank Rate, IADB money/credit/mortgages, SONIA with specific terms, BoE gilt curves, DMO auctions, ONS public finances and HM Treasury | ONS trade and balance of payments, DESNZ Energy Trends, ONS population |
| Canada | StatCan CPI and core variants, quarterly expenditure GDP and monthly GDP by industry, LFS, payroll and vacancies | StatCan retail, manufacturing sales, permits, new house prices, household spending. | Italy | ISTAT national NIC/FOI and HICP, quarterly accounts, labour force, wages and productivity | ISTAT industrial production, retail, confidence, permits and housing prices | Banca d’Italia BDS bank rates/credit, benchmarks and Rendistato, MEF auctions and debt. ECB common policy | ISTAT trade and population, Banca d’Italia balance of payments, Terna power and MASE energy (verify product terms) |

France, Germany and Italy share ECB monetary policy. A national CPI, Eurostat HICP and country-specific inflation exclusions are distinct series variants. Eurostat owns the harmonised dissemination layer, with national sources in metadata. ECB euro-area curve composition and the euro-area aggregate must be versioned separately from national sovereigns.

## Trading Economics benchmark

Its API exposes historical indicators, calendar releases, revisions and discontinued series, markets and proprietary forecasts. The published schemas carry source and source URL fields for indicator/calendar records. Use that as a coverage benchmark and cross-check leads with the actual producer. The documentation does not establish the upstream collection method or licensing chain for every market quote. Keep release actual, prior, revised prior, reference period and consensus distinct. Track calendar release events and values independently. Additional useful categories include capacity utilisation, bankruptcies, business inventories, government cash balances, imports/exports by product, tourism, productivity, and job vacancy rates. Forecasts, consensus and credit ratings need separate source and rights work.
