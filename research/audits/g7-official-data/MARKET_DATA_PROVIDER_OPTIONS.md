# Market data provider options

| Need | Candidate | What it measures | Cost and paid-product status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad dollar | Federal Reserve H.10 nominal broad dollar index | Trade-weighted Fed index, daily. Existing `frb_broad_usd_daily` already provides the distinct concept | Public download, no data-access fee identified. Eligible Board data with attribution. Different weights and base from ICE USDX |
| FX | ECB reference FX, eligible Fed H.10, BoC Valet FX | Daily official reference/fixing rates, no executable quote | ECB/Fed exact eligible rows are strongest. BoC exact terms check pending. No paid feed required for those official reference rates |
| WTI and Brent | EIA Cushing WTI and Europe Brent spot | Daily spot reference history, USD/barrel. Reporting delay follows EIA publication | Public files; API key for EIA. EIA reuse terms subject to source/third-party checks. Separate IDs from continuous futures |
| Gold / silver | LBMA benchmarks administered by IBA; CME COMEX futures as distinct products | Auction benchmark versus settlement/continuous futures. Roll and settlement conventions matter | Licence and use-specific quote. Public visibility or historical downloads alone do not establish rights |
| Equity indices | S&P DJI for S&P 500, FTSE Russell, STOXX, Nikkei, or licensed distributor | Index points, price versus total-return variant separately | Quote for external distribution and index owner rights. S&P 500 availability through a feed does not by itself cover paid display |
| Volatility | Cboe VIX licensing | Cboe index history, daily close available as a research reference | Quote/confirmation for paid display, archival redistribution and derived use. Realized volatility is a different metric and inherits input rights |
| USDX | ICE market data | Proprietary fixed-basket US Dollar Index | Quote/confirmation. Fed broad dollar is a separately labelled product |
| Energy and metals futures | CME/NYMEX and ICE | Contract-level exchange data; fixed rolls or individual contracts | Exchange/vendor contract and applicable display, delayed, historical, derived and non-display schedules |
| Crypto | CoinGecko business/custom or exchange contract | Aggregated token-level quotes versus individual venue trades, frequency and lookback tier-dependent | Standard commercial plans and custom rights differ. Redistribution/resale and white labelling require review. Defer unless needed |
| Multiple markets | Twelve Data Business, Massive Business, Trading Economics | Coverage, delays and backfill vary by dataset, exchange and entitlement | Twelve Data advertises business external distribution offerings. Massive standard market terms restrict onward display absent permission. Trading Economics prices depend on features, request volume and distribution. Paid SaaS costs and covered instruments require written quotes |

## Product decision and quotes

**Exclude Yahoo Finance / yfinance** from the proposed paid production sources. Existing instruments are `^GSPC`, `GC=F`, `CL=F`, `DX-Y.NYB`. Preserve their IDs and the semantics of saved investigations. A paid release can use separately labelled Fed broad dollar, approved reference FX and EIA spot price IDs. Keep exchange/index products pending an instrument-level contract. Do not overwrite futures history with spot data or treat public real-economy indicators as market quotes.

Collect a written quote for the exact symbols and vintages, day/session close or intraday delay, earliest usable observations, exchanges and territories, external authenticated display, downstream chart data, bulk file/API export, retention and termination, model processing/training, alerting, derived stats and applicable attribution. Verify whether usage is per user, professional/non-professional, account, device, API volume, exchange, or enterprise minimum. Public list prices for generic plans are not verified total costs for MacroLens rights. No market-data subscription was purchased.
