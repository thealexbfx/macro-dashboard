# Commercial data rights ledger

A means evidence supports the intended commercial display of the exact producer-owned selection subject to the stated conditions. B requires clarification or contract review. C excludes the current route for the paid product. D means insufficient evidence. A is a scoped evidence classification. Product-level download, AI transfer/training, and any use beyond the stated source terms require separate review.

## Required per-series decision

The CSV provides the dataset-level evidence link, scope, owner, attribution and provisional classifications. For each product surface record yes, conditional, no or unknown for commercial display, public redistribution, cache/backfill duration, bulk export, analytics outputs, internal machine processing, external model prompts and training. Approval for internal storage or AI processing does not confer approval for sharing the values. Treat export and non-display calculations as separate use cases. Record terms version, series selection, review date, territory, expiry and contract or permission evidence. Keep a defined owner and evidence for each source input. Unknown or restricted rights stay unavailable to the corresponding surface.
