# MacroLens V1 Product Scope

## Public navigation
- Ask MacroLens
- Markets
- Macro
- How It Works

## Core customer promise
MacroLens helps users understand what is happening in markets and the macroeconomy by combining trusted data, MacroLens models, historical analysis, targeted current-event research, and transparent evidence.

## Launch capabilities
1. Ask MacroLens
   - factual economic lookups
   - market lookups
   - market-period calculations
   - macro analysis
   - selected current-market investigations
   - historical relationship research
2. Markets
   - Gold, S&P 500, Nasdaq, DXY, WTI
   - US 2Y, 10Y, 30Y
   - EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY
3. Macro
   - United States, United Kingdom, Euro Area, Japan
   - Growth, Inflation, Labour, Liquidity, Policy
4. How It Works
   - methodology, sources, limitations, confidence

## Explicitly not launch requirements
- broad crypto coverage
- every global bond market
- minute-perfect event attribution
- professional terminal-style news feed
- standalone Regime Explorer navigation
- standalone Research Workspace / Active Ideas for customers
- expensive institutional market-data contracts before demand is proven

## Product rules
- The answer page must contain enough information to understand the answer without forcing navigation.
- MacroLens features appear contextually only when they materially improve the user's research.
- Simple questions receive simple answers; complex questions can trigger investigations.
- LLMs may interpret and explain evidence but must not invent market/economic observations or unsupported causal conclusions.
- Uncertainty and data limitations are surfaced explicitly.
- Initial infrastructure target: stay below roughly $100/month until demand justifies more.
